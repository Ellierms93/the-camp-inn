OUR LITTLE PATCH

A campsite built around the way we like to camp.

Not just somewhere to park up and disappear behind a caravan door. The Camp Inn is being created as a relaxed little countryside community: plenty of space, animals to meet, good food, muddy boots and evenings that last until the light goes.

No-faff pricing. Up to six people included, with dogs, awnings and pup tents included where permitted.
